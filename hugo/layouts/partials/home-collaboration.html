REALTIME DIAGRAM COLLABORATION

A shared diagram.
Approved people.

Work together from computers, tablets and phones. See who is connected and which nodes they are exploring, while each person keeps a separate local workspace.

  1. 01

    Invite, review, then work together

    The owner approves each device as an Editor or Viewer. Editors can change the diagram; Viewers can explore it. An invitation alone does not grant access.

  2. 02

    Encrypted between approved devices

    Shared messages use MLS (RFC 9420) encryption. The relay forwards encrypted messages; approved participants can read the content you choose to share.

  3. 03

    Your local workspace stays yours

    Saved records remain protected by AES-256-GCM in each browser. Cameras and personal settings stay local. Source metadata, owner profiles and raw datasets need explicit disclosure.

Where does an AI agent fit?

An agent uses an approved participant’s local API/MCP connection. Its edits need both that participant’s room role and an explicit local write grant. A Viewer stays read-only; the agent cannot invite or approve people.
