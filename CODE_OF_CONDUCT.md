# Code of Conduct

Visual Nerve welcomes people with different backgrounds and experience levels.
These expectations apply to project issues, pull requests, reviews and other
project community interactions.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and patient. Explain assumptions and help others reproduce problems.
- Discuss the work and its effects; keep criticism constructive.
- Respect differing experience, language and accessibility needs.
- Accept corrections and the maintainer's scope and review decisions.
- Protect personal information, private diagrams and credentials.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized comments, personal attacks,
publishing someone else's private information and disruptive spam are not
acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Project maintainer [Johan Caripson](https://github.com/Caripson) handles reports.
Email [hello@visualnerve.com](mailto:hello@visualnerve.com) for sensitive conduct
reports; the [support guide](SUPPORT.md#private-contact) explains private contact.
Do not publish personal information or allegations in an ordinary issue.
Security vulnerabilities have a separate route in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

The maintainer may request a change in behavior, remove harmful content, close
disruptive threads or limit participation according to severity and repetition.
Reports are reviewed with respect for the people involved; information is shared
only as needed to investigate and act. There is no promised response deadline.
